**Meeting Name:** SRE CoDev Monthly Council Meeting  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Frequency:** Monthly  
**Participants:** Council Chair, Core Members, Advisors, Administrative Support  
**Facilitator:** Council Chair or Designated Moderator  
**Timekeeper:** Assigned member to ensure adherence to time blocks  
**Documenter:** Administrative Support or assigned member for minutes  

---

### **Meeting Agenda**

| **Time** | **Agenda Item**       | **Duration** | **Owner**          | **Purpose**              |
|----------|------------------------|-------------|--------------------|--------------------------|
| **0:00–0:05** | **Welcome & Objectives** | 5 mins      | Council Chair    | Align on goals & set focus for the meeting. |
| **0:05–0:15** | **Review Action Items from Last Meeting** | 10 mins     | Administrative Support | Brief review of action items and updates. |
| **0:15–0:30** | **Project Updates and Metrics Review** | 15 mins     | Project Leads    | Review ongoing initiatives and key metrics (e.g., reliability, time-to-market). |
| **0:30–0:45** | **Discussion & Decision-Making** | 15 mins     | Council Chair    | Deep dive into proposals, challenges, and voting on key decisions. |
| **0:45–0:55** | **Prioritization & Next Steps** | 10 mins     | Council Chair    | Use prioritization rubric to assess new opportunities. |
| **0:55–1:00** | **Action Items & Closing Remarks** | 5 mins      | Council Chair    | Summarize action items, assign responsibilities, and set next meeting goals. |

---

### **Detailed Breakdown**

#### **1. Welcome & Objectives (5 mins)**  
- Quick roll call and acknowledgment of participants.  
- Outline the main focus areas of the meeting.  
- Reiterate the desired outcomes (e.g., decisions, approvals, alignment).  

**Example Script:**  
*"Welcome everyone. Today we’ll focus on reviewing our key metrics, discussing two new co-development opportunities, and voting on one high-priority proposal."*

---

#### **2. Review Action Items from Last Meeting (10 mins)**  
- Review pending action items from the previous meeting.  
- Confirm which tasks are completed, in progress, or blocked.  
- Assign follow-ups if necessary.  

**Example Action Item Review Template:**  

| **Action Item** | **Owner** | **Status** | **Notes** |
|------------------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Finalize Project X Proposal | Alice      | Complete   | Approved last week. |
| Metrics Dashboard Update   | Bob        | In Progress | Awaiting data feed. |

---

#### **3. Project Updates and Metrics Review (15 mins)**  
- **Objective:** Understand the status of ongoing projects and critical performance metrics.  
- Focus on key metrics: Uptime, MTBF, MTTR, error rates.  
- Highlight achievements and challenges.  

**Example Metrics Table:**  

| **Metric**        | **Target** | **Actual** | **Variance** | **Notes**   |
|--------------------|-----------|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| Availability (%)  | 99.9%     | 99.85%    | -0.05%      | Issue on 5/12 resolved. |
| MTTR (hours)      | 1.0       | 1.2       | +0.2        | Investigating delay. |

---

#### **4. Discussion & Decision-Making (15 mins)**  
- **Objective:** Focused discussion on key challenges, risks, or opportunities.  
- Present prioritized proposals for approval or rejection.  
- Ensure transparency in decisions.  
- Use the **Prioritization Rubric** to evaluate proposals (e.g., Business Value, Feasibility).  

**Example Proposal Format:**  
- **Proposal Name:** Automated Alerting System Integration  
- **Owner:** Team Lead X  
- **Score (via Prioritization Rubric):** 4.5/5  
- **Decision Required:** Approval to proceed  

**Voting Method:** Consensus-driven or anonymous ballot if needed.  

---

#### **5. Prioritization & Next Steps (10 mins)**  
- **Objective:** Rank new opportunities using the **Prioritization Rubric**.  
- Ensure alignment with quarterly objectives and strategic goals.  
- Assign owners and set deadlines.  

**Example Prioritization Summary:**  

| **Project Name**   | **Score** | **Priority** | **Owner** |
|---------------------|---------|-------------|---------|
| Observability Dashboard | 4.7     | High        | Team A   |
| Incident Automation    | 4.2     | Medium      | Team B   |

---

#### **6. Action Items & Closing Remarks (5 mins)**  
- Recap all agreed-upon actions and responsible parties.  
- Confirm deadlines.  
- Close with brief feedback or reflections on meeting effectiveness.  

**Example Action Item Table:**  

| **Task**               | **Owner** | **Deadline** |
|-------------------------|-----------|-------------|
| Prepare draft proposal for Incident Automation | Alice    | Next Meeting |
| Investigate MTTR delay | Bob      | 2 Weeks     |
