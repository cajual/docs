## Formal Overview: Enterprise Contribution Forum for Software Engineers

**1. Introduction**

This document outlines the operational framework for a voluntary, self-organized forum designed to facilitate and promote software engineer participation in enterprise-wide contribution opportunities, including CoDev and innersourcing initiatives. The forum aims to foster a collaborative environment conducive to networking, innovation, and impactful project engagement.

**2. Objectives**

* To provide a low-friction platform for software engineers to engage in enterprise contribution opportunities.
* To facilitate the formation of self-governed teams for collaborative project execution.
* To promote knowledge sharing and cross-functional collaboration within the engineering community.
* To enhance the visibility and impact of innersourcing and CoDev initiatives.
* To empower engineers to drive innovation and contribute to strategic organizational goals.

**3. Operational Structure**

* **Participation:** Participation in the forum is entirely voluntary and open to all software engineers.
* **Team Formation:** Engineers will self-organize into teams of 4-7 members based on shared interests and project alignment.
* **Team Governance:** Each team will be self-governed, with an appointed lead or accountable engineer responsible for project management and progress.
* **Communication:** Each team will elect a dedicated communicator to facilitate information exchange with the broader forum.
* **Champion Role:** A group of designated champions will be responsible for:
    * Identifying and highlighting relevant innersourcing and CoDev opportunities.
    * Collaborating with the Bank Tech Engineering Champions group to align on priority initiatives.
    * Publishing a monthly review of forum accomplishments.
    * Managing administrative tasks, including Jira, Confluence, and Slack.
* **Opportunity Sourcing:** Teams are encouraged to identify and propose their own project opportunities in addition to those provided by the champions.

**4. Communication and Collaboration**

* **Asynchronous Communication:** The forum will operate asynchronously, with all communication conducted through a dedicated Slack channel.
* **Knowledge Sharing:** Confluence will serve as a central repository for information sharing, including project documentation, resources, and team profiles.
* **Project Tracking:** Jira will be utilized for tracking project progress, effort, and issue management.

**5. Administrative Framework**

* **Champion Responsibilities:** Champions will manage the forum's administrative infrastructure, including:
    * Maintaining Jira boards for opportunity and effort tracking.
    * Curating and updating Confluence pages for information dissemination.
    * Managing the dedicated Slack channel for communication.
* **Low-Touch Approach:** The administrative framework will be designed to minimize overhead and maximize engineer autonomy.

**6. Reporting and Recognition**

* **Monthly Review:** Champions will publish a monthly review summarizing the accomplishments and contributions of participating teams.
* **Recognition:** The forum will strive to recognize and celebrate the contributions of participating engineers and teams.

**7. Expected Outcomes**

* Increased participation in enterprise contribution initiatives.
* Enhanced collaboration and knowledge sharing among software engineers.
* Accelerated innovation and project delivery.
* Improved visibility and impact of innersourcing and CoDev projects.
* Increased engineer engagement and professional development.
