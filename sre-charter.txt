SRE CoDev Council Charter
1. Purpose: The Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) CoDev Council serves as a central governing body to identify, prioritize, and oversee co-development (CoDev) opportunities across the enterprise. The council aims to enhance collaboration between the SRE organization and external partners by identifying high-impact opportunities for joint development efforts and facilitating the submission of SRE-led projects to external stakeholders through the CoDev platform.
2. Mission Statement: Empower the SRE organization to drive engineering excellence through strategic co-development initiatives, fostering innovation, improving system reliability, and accelerating value delivery by collaborating with internal and external engineering teams.
3. Objectives:
Opportunity Identification: Proactively identify and evaluate high-priority co-development opportunities across the enterprise.
Prioritization Framework: Establish a standardized framework for assessing the value, feasibility, and alignment of potential CoDev initiatives.
Engagement Facilitation: Act as a liaison between SRE teams and external partners for co-development activities.
Seeding Opportunities: Create a structured process for SRE teams to propose projects and seed them through the CoDev platform.
Knowledge Sharing: Promote the sharing of best practices, lessons learned, and reusable assets across SRE and partner organizations.
Governance and Accountability: Ensure transparency, measurable outcomes, and continuous improvement in all CoDev initiatives.
4. Scope of Work:
Identify strategic CoDev opportunities aligned with business and technical goals.
Facilitate project onboarding, execution, and delivery processes.
Oversee project governance, ensuring adherence to established quality and performance metrics.
Act as the approval authority for SRE-led projects submitted to the CoDev platform.
Build and maintain strong relationships with external CoDev partners.
5. Council Structure:
Council Chair: Senior leader from the SRE organization.
Core Members: Representatives from key SRE teams, product management, and engineering leadership.
Advisors: Domain experts, technical architects, and operational leads.
Administrative Support: Program management office (PMO) or designated operations support.
6. Decision-Making Process:
Decisions will be made based on a consensus-driven approach.
High-priority projects will be evaluated using a formal prioritization rubric.
Regular review meetings will ensure alignment with strategic objectives.
7. Key Responsibilities:
Develop and maintain a CoDev opportunity backlog.
Conduct quarterly reviews of ongoing and proposed CoDev projects.
Measure and report on the success and outcomes of CoDev initiatives.
Provide guidance and mentorship to SRE teams engaging in CoDev projects.
8. Governance and Reporting:
Bi-annual progress reports will be shared with senior leadership.
Metrics will include project completion rates, partner satisfaction, and value delivered.
Continuous feedback loops will ensure alignment with evolving organizational priorities.
9. Meetings and Cadence:
Monthly Meetings: Focused on progress updates, project prioritization, and issue resolution.
Quarterly Reviews: Strategic assessment of outcomes and roadmap alignment.
Ad-Hoc Meetings: As required for urgent opportunities or escalations.
10. Success Metrics:
Number of high-priority CoDev projects initiated and completed.
Reduction in time-to-market for collaborative projects.
Improved reliability and scalability of systems through joint efforts.
Positive feedback from CoDev partners and stakeholders.
11. Communication and Transparency:
Council decisions and updates will be documented and shared with relevant stakeholders.
A centralized knowledge repository will host documentation, best practices, and case studies.
12. Continuous Improvement: The council will periodically review its charter, objectives, and processes to ensure ongoing alignment with business and technical goals.
